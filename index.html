Research & Innovation

ECG is All You Need [Clinical Study — n=311]

Co-Investigator
  • Demonstrated first proof-of-concept for cross-modal cardiac inference: LLM-generated echocardiographic reports from 12-lead ECG images alone, without fine-tuning.
  • Retrospective analysis of 311 paired ECG–echocardiogram cases comparing three LLMs against cardiologist gold standard.
  • Best model achieved r=0.394 (p<0.001) for ejection fraction prediction, with 64% of estimates within ±5% of true values.
  • Aimed at democratizing cardiac imaging in resource-constrained settings where echocardiography is unavailable.

Screening for Left Ventricular Dysfunction from Photographed 12-Lead ECGs [Deep Learning — n=500]

Co-Investigator
  • Trained a multi-task EfficientNet-B3 ensemble to predict ejection fraction and regional wall-motion abnormality directly from photographed 12-lead ECGs.
  • Three-stage transfer learning (ImageNet → PTB-XL 21,430 ECGs → 500 paired records) with inverse-density loss weighting and out-of-fold cross-validation.
  • Severe-EF detection AUROC 0.76, RWMA screen AUROC 0.70; best folds reach 0.83–0.86. Diagnosed and fixed shortcut-learning and regression-to-the-mean failure modes via Grad-CAM.
  • Deployed as a multilingual WhatsApp triage tool with a public Hugging Face demo.

HemoglobinAI [Clinical Study – n=600]

Principal Investigator
  • Developed a multimodal machine learning pipeline for non-invasive hemoglobin estimation using smartphone imaging.
  • Compared palpebral conjunctiva and buccal mucosa images in 600 pregnant women using standardized color calibration.
  • Implemented YOLOv8-based segmentation and evaluated seven regression models with 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Demonstrated marginally superior predictive performance using palpebral conjunctiva imaging.

Hobby Projects

Matali

An ambulance routing simulation. It looks at travel time, hospital capacity and the type of injury to work out where to send a patient. Still a simulation.

Diphea

Working on an Android app that measures how you touch and move your phone. It turns those movements into features and compares them with your own baseline. A work in progress.

Neula

A desktop experiment with typing rhythm and mouse movements. It looks at timing and movement patterns, learns a personal baseline and flags changes. The data stays on your computer.

Spectral Manifold

A small experiment with sound and 3D visuals. It takes audio from the microphone and makes the shapes move with it.
