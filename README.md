## Writing

The portfolio links to `/writing/`, a collection of personal essays and short stories. Each piece has its own page:

- `writing/question/index.html`: the essay titled `?`.
- `writing/sacred-realms/index.html`: the short story *Sacred Realms*.
- `writing/writing.css`: shared reading-page styles.

The pieces retain the author's original wording and spelling. The essay has paragraph breaks added for readability, and its supplied excerpt appears beneath its title on the writing index. No publication dates are inferred from dates mentioned within the story.

To add a piece, copy an existing article into a new folder under `writing/`, update its title, description, category, and body, then add a linked entry to `writing/index.html`. Escape HTML characters in text (`&` as `&amp;`, `<` as `&lt;`, and `>` as `&gt;`). Relative links and ordinary HTML/CSS keep the section compatible with GitHub Pages; no build step is needed.
