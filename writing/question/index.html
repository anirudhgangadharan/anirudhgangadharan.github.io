Personal essay

?

By Anirudh Gangadharan

I feel there is no need to do anything, what is the need for desire. It is not that I want to do easy work, I am willing to work hard, I am willing to work on things that I donot like in places that I donot like, but deep down I ask myself , what the point of all of this is, what all of this means. Liberation from the cycles of karma- birth and death is what I am really after. I am depressed, and low energy, I donot know where I am going. I have surrendered myself to godess kali devi. I donot need anything more, anymore. I am done with life.

Nature itself is the illusion , we are play things in the mind of god. I know for a fact that I can meddle with Maya and change reality in ways that benefit me, or ways that I desire. But what is the point, it is maya at the end of the day. Who is the one desiring, is it really me, or is it nature. Do I consent to that? Do I allow my body and mind to pursue the goal nature has set for me? Who wants to propagate the genes, is it me or nature? Who is trying to understand itself? me or nature?

It is not sadness, nor happiness, it is beyond both, beyond all polarities of life. Beyond all of that there exists the truth, the unchanged. I seek to reach that. I wonder how it would be like to feel god? Maybe , maybe in some life, I could be the rain, and the wind , the seed that sprouts from the dung of catlle, I could be the dung and the fly, the sun and the sunlight, the emotions that humans experience. I wonder what it is to be the joy and sorrow one experiences, what it would mean to be the experience itself.

I consider this identity of mine an illusion. Who am I really? If not for the emergent property of the trillion cells interacting in my body, who am I? I am memory of whatever I think I am, or whatever I remember myself to be. Who am I , if I am not for the assumptions people, places and events have of me, compelling, influencing and guiding me to act in ways that satisfy their unconscious assumptions.

The thought patterns that I have are deemed to be wrong by some, but why do I need to accept their verdict? What experience of life do they have to give a judgement that defines their worldview as correct? Again that would be the unconscious assumption of a group that deems that it is correct, a collective hallucination, that they call reality.

There is a tendency deep within me, to even validate these thoughts. But do they cease to be true just because it is not validated? Do these thoughts become true and carry weight just because someone validated it? Even the very act of someone validating this would depend on my unconscious assumption that it would be validated. Doubts creeping up in my mind would psychically influence the degree to which one would believe my statements and thereby validation that it would receive. Strong enough, they would be a mirror or whatever I assume my ideas or thoughts to be.

So my question to you is, if my own unconscious assumptions can influence how my ideas are perceived, will playing with maya in a way to have my ideas accepted mess with reality? Do I allow that?

I love ranting and sharing my thoughts, in hopes that one day, people would see that I am right, or someone would see what I meant. Alas, those are hopes and hope is what is driving me to type these letters. Do I really want to be seen for who I am or is this authenticity and vulnerability just a mask to gain acceptance.

?
